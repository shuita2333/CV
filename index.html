    About 关于

    I am Yuanhe Zhang, a Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science (National Pilot Software Engineering School) at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications. I am a member of the State Key Laboratory of Networking and Switching Technology, advised by Prof. Sen Su.

    My research focuses on the safety and reliability of large language models and LLM-based agents. In particular, I study resource-consumption attacks and defenses, emerging risks in multi-agent and Model Context Protocol systems, and robustness in large audio and vision-language models.

    I am currently seeking research internship opportunities and am open to research collaborations in trustworthy language models, multimodal models, and agent safety.

    我是张原赫（Yuanhe Zhang），现为北京邮电大学计算机学院（国家示范性软件学院）博士研究生，就读于网络与交换技术全国重点实验室，导师为苏森教授。

    我的研究聚焦于大语言模型及大语言模型智能体的安全性、可靠性与可信部署，主要包括大模型资源消耗攻击与防御、多智能体与模型上下文协议（MCP）系统中的新型安全风险，以及大音频语言模型和大视觉语言模型的鲁棒性。

    我目前正在寻找研究型实习机会，并开放大语言模型、多模态模型与智能体安全方向的科研合作。

    Research 研究方向

    • LLM safety: resource-consumption attacks, denial-of-service risks, and deployable defenses.
    • Agent safety: backdoors, behavioral misalignment, multi-agent threats, and adaptive trust calibration for MCP agents.
    • Multimodal robustness: trustworthy large audio language models and large vision-language models under noise and adversarial perturbations.
    • LLM evaluation: reliability, length instruction following, and safety-oriented red teaming.
    • 大语言模型安全：资源消耗攻击、拒绝服务风险与可部署防御方法。
    • 智能体安全：后门攻击、行为失配、多智能体威胁与 MCP 智能体的自适应信任校准。
    • 多模态鲁棒性：大音频语言模型和大视觉语言模型在噪声及对抗扰动下的可信性。
    • 大语言模型评测：模型可靠性、长度指令遵循与面向安全的红队测试。
