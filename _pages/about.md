    <span data-lang="en">About</span>
    <span data-lang="zh" hidden>关于</span>
  </h1>

  <div data-lang="en">
    <p>I am <strong>Yuanhe Zhang</strong>, a Ph.D. student in the <a href="https://scs.bupt.edu.cn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">School of Computer Science (National Pilot Software Engineering School)</a> at <a href="https://www.bupt.edu.cn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications</a>. <section id="research" class="cv-section anchor-section" aria-labelledby="research-heading">
  <h1 id="research-heading">
    <span data-lang="en">Research</span>
    <span data-lang="zh" hidden>研究方向</span>
  </h1>

  <div data-lang="en">
    <ul class="research-list">
      <li><strong>LLM safety:</strong> resource-consumption attacks, denial-of-service risks, and deployable defenses.</li>
      <li><strong>Agent safety:</strong> backdoors, behavioral misalignment, multi-agent threats, and adaptive trust calibration for MCP agents.</li>
      <li><strong>Multimodal robustness:</strong> trustworthy large audio language models and large vision-language models under noise and adversarial perturbations.</li>
      <li><strong>LLM evaluation:</strong> reliability, length instruction following, and safety-oriented red teaming.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div data-lang="zh" hidden>
    <ul class="research-list">
      <li><strong>大语言模型安全：</strong>资源消耗攻击、拒绝服务风险与可部署防御方法。</li>
      <li><strong>智能体安全：</strong>后门攻击、行为失配、多智能体威胁与 MCP 智能体的自适应信任校准。</li>
      <li><strong>多模态鲁棒性：</strong>大音频语言模型和大视觉语言模型在噪声及对抗扰动下的可信性。</li>
      <li><strong>大语言模型评测：</strong>模型可靠性、长度指令遵循与面向安全的红队测试。</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</section>
