My research examines security and reliability risks that emerge when large language models are deployed as services or embedded in autonomous agents.

Current themes include:

- Resource-consumption attacks and defenses for LLM services
- Backdoor and behavioral risks in LLM-based agents
- Trust calibration for Model Context Protocol agents
- Robustness of large audio and vision-language models
